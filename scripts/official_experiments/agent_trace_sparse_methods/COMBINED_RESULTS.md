# Agent trace serving: SWE-lite and Gasai

Both workloads replay complete agent trajectories with recorded answer tokens
forced at the sampling boundary and deterministic synthetic tool waits in
`[0, 2)` seconds (seed 42). The metric is the complete client replay time
after server readiness, including waits and scheduling; model loading and CUDA
Graph capture are excluded. It does not measure task quality. All measurements
used the same two NVIDIA H20 GPUs, TP2/EP2/DP1, CUDA Graph, and
`max_decoding_seqs=max_num_seqs_in_batch=C` with
`max_num_seqs_in_gpu=1.5*C`.

| Workload | Replay model | Agents | Requests | Forced output tokens | Trace manifest SHA-256 |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| SWE-lite longest appendable trace | GLM-4.7-Flash | 100 | 8,000 | 1,777,061 | `e9449fda…a60a0b001` |
| Gasai seed-42 random trace | Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507-FP8 | 100 | 5,210 | 1,036,727 | `4126a043…e6ec455c` |

The two sections use different models and trajectories. Their wall times and
throughput rates should not be compared across sections. The ratio column is
**Vanilla replay time divided by the case replay time within that section**;
values above 1 mean a shorter replay. `*` denotes a different concurrency
from that section's Vanilla baseline, so it is a configuration comparison,
not an isolated sparse-method speedup. `P50/P95/P99` are non-streaming HTTP
request latencies in seconds.

## SWE-lite trace · GLM-4.7-Flash

The 100 appendable trajectories have 80 requests each. The recorded trajectory
was generated by GLM-4.7-Flash. [Detailed protocol and validity](RESULTS.md)
and [per-case launch arguments and metrics](results.json).

| Vanilla · radix prefix | 40 | 60 | 5,884.06 | 302.01 | 10.53 / 81.26 / 141.98 | 7,999 | 1.00× |
| QuEST · radix prefix · 2K | 32 | 48 | 5,530.32 | 321.33 | 7.86 / 58.60 / 103.20 | 7,999 | 1.06×* |
| OmniKV · radix prefix · 2K | 40 | 60 | 3,745.13 | 474.50 | 6.40 / 48.91 / 86.47 | 7,999 | **1.57×** |
| SnapKV · Chain Cache · 16K · decode eviction | 52 | 78 | 4,919.70 | 361.21 | 11.43 / 88.52 / 157.62 | 7,900 | 1.20×* |
| H2O · Chain Cache · probability · decode eviction | 80 | 120 | 4,250.30 | 418.10 | 14.06 / 108.01 / 186.65 | 7,900 | 1.38×* |

The two H2O C80 rows differ in prefill chunk size and
`favor_min_decoding_seqs` as well as score mode and decode eviction. Their
ratio cannot be assigned to one parameter. QuEST C40 completed the trace but
had 13 active-request preemptions. SnapKV C80 without decode eviction failed
with capacity errors; its decode-eviction C80 retry had preemptions and request
timeouts. These attempts have no accepted throughput point. The valid QuEST
C32 and SnapKV C52 reruns are listed above.

## Gasai trace · Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507-FP8

The 100 trajectories were sampled with seed 42. [Detailed protocol and
validity](GASAI_QWEN3_FP8_RESULTS.md) and
[per-case launch arguments and metrics](gasai_qwen3_fp8_results.json).

| Method and effective setting | C | GPU rows | Replay (s) | Output tok/s | P50/P95/P99 (s) | Cache-hit requests | Time ratio vs Vanilla C32 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Vanilla · radix prefix | 32 | 48 | 2,993.70 | 346.30 | 8.24 / 50.46 / 117.74 | 5,209 | 1.00× |
| QuEST · radix prefix · 2K | 32 | 48 | 3,532.43 | 293.49 | 9.57 / 57.85 / 134.92 | 5,209 | **0.85×** |
| OmniKV · radix prefix · 2K | 32 | 48 | 1,837.36 | 564.25 | 4.75 / 29.95 / 68.73 | 5,209 | **1.63×** |
| SnapKV · Chain Cache · 16K · decode eviction | 52 | 78 | 1,800.23 | 575.88 | 7.71 / 47.00 / 114.10 | 5,110 | 1.66×* |
| SnapKV · Chain Cache · 8K · decode eviction | 72 | 108 | 1,333.80 | 777.27 | 7.23 / 42.72 / 105.04 | 5,110 | 2.24×* |
| H2O · Chain Cache · probability · decode eviction | 80 | 120 | 1,502.06 | 690.20 | 9.89 / 55.89 / 128.90 | 5,110 | 1.99×* |

An earlier SnapKV 16K C32 diagnostic completed the same Gasai trace at
1,832.04 s and 565.89 output token/s, with 5,110 cache-hit requests and zero
preemptions. Its 1.63× ratio uses Vanilla C32 at matching concurrency. The
requested 16K result is C52 in the main table; the C32 result is recorded
separately under `diagnostic_results` in the Qwen result JSON.

QuEST, OmniKV, and H2O in the Qwen run used an isolated checkout with an
extended Qwen3-MoE TP2/EP2 prefix-cache validation gate. They passed GPU
smoke and complete replay on this tested topology; the gate extension is not
a general support claim. H2O here uses probability scoring with decode
eviction, not the SWE-lite FP32-logits/no-eviction variant.

Every listed run completed its workload with the stated output count, observed
prefix or chain reuse, zero active-request preemptions, and zero recompute
replays. Each full server log has two rank-level CUDA Graph capture-complete
records, the expected request-finish count, and no recorded Graph fallback or
error. The machine-readable result files retain GPU UUIDs, exact engine
settings, trace and forced-workload digests, Git commit, and artifact roots.

QuEST's 2K is its selected token budget after the first two full layers;
OmniKV's 2K applies to its sparse layers, with full layers selected by `auto`.
SnapKV budgets are retained token totals. H2O has a 16K prefill and 8K decode
budget in both variants.
